Connecting people through play

QuestLinq started behind the microphone. Our founders have spent years running trivia and music bingo nights in Kingston as Fat City Entertainment — watching empty weeknights turn into rooms that regulars plan their week around.

The lesson from all those nights: the magic isn't the format, it's the doorway. Give people a reason to walk through a door together — a mystery to solve, a quest to run, a song to butcher — and strangers become regulars.

QuestLinq opens that doorway wider than one crew ever could:

  • Guests find free and ticketed event nights near them.
  • Venues find the hosts who fill rooms — and connect for less than the cost of a round of drinks.
  • Hosts get found, get booked, and never pay for leads.
  • And anyone can grab a printable host pack and run the night themselves.

We're starting where we live — Kingston, Ontario — and expanding city by city. If you run a room, run a show, or just need a better Tuesday: welcome in.
