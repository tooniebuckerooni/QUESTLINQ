## 4. On Hold — Trivia / Game Show Night ⏸

The chat's SKU #4 (trivia rounds, Feud-style surveys, scoring sheets) is **not a launch product**: selling trivia kits competes directly with Fat City Entertainment's core hosted-trivia business.

Resolution options, in preference order:
1. **Rebrand as "Game Show Night" only** — Feud-style surveys, buzzer games, no pub-trivia question rounds (probably safe; revisit with Fat City hat on).
2. **Geo-hold** — release only outside Fat City's operating market once expansion begins.
3. **Shelve** until Fat City's positioning changes.

*(Listing QuestLinq events that are trivia nights — including Fat City's — remains fine; the conflict is only in selling DIY trivia kits.)*
